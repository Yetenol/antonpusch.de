# AFK

If you are hand crafting something that takes so long you feel the urge to leave it running while it finishes then you're playing wrong. Automate instead. Or, in a pinch like for that MK2 armor,  drop a few factories down and hand fill them to make mk1 and mk2 modules while also hand crafting tier 2 modules. 
The answer isn't more time, it's more factories and more production. That is why "the factory must grow" is our motto

# Huge buffers

This a manufacturing pitfall because a large buffer, like above, is using time instead of production. But it's really just a bandaid for the answer which is (all together now) "The factory must grow" if you make 2k green circuits/min and use 5k/min a buffer will not fix the fact you are running at -3k/min. In fact, a buffer will mean you factory looks like it's working great when really you're just delaying the moment your factory collapses. Often you'll actually start using more green chips because you think everything is fine. Running out of buffer faster  because you really need to make more green circuits! 
Inevitability 1- make more green circuits.
Option- make a small buffer (6 or less boxes) with an alarm if the count drops below mostly full
Suggestion- make your buffets with 1:1 splitters off then back on so that the main line isn't interrupted.

# Not using robots to build.

Unless you are challenging yourself or just plain crazy, use robots! Use copy-paste, use deconstruct, use upgrade planners and use robo ports! 

I've played just about every factory sim game there is and nothing comes close to the way Factorio moves beyond the limit of one man.
Mindustry, Minecraft, Satisfactory, Shapes.io, Hydroneer, Dyson Sphere, Oxygen Not Included, they all have their own special place in my heart but none of them allow you to graduate from walking around the map doing all the work yourself! This is the reason that I keep coming back to Factorio over and over again. Once you reach robots and get their speed up a few times you only have to make each thing one time! Then copy paste. And the better you are at making blueprints, the less time you spend connecting them to your base. 

# Using other people's blueprints.

Remember when you discovered cheat codes in Age of Empires and drove around the map with a bazooka? Fun right? Until it wasn't about 1hr later because there was no challenge left. Ok maybe you're too young for that or "power overwhelming" but the point is to challenge yourself. If you take someone else's BP and plop it down you've just robbed yourself of hours of the best funny this game has to offer. Don't be afraid of others BPs but use them as inspiration or learning. Try your own and ask for help if you need. Another of the most fun part of the game is helping each other succeed. Give us that chance.

# Hand crafting

this isnt as much "you're doing it wrong" as much as you aren't doing it right. If you think it'll take too much time to automate red belts or underground belts or inserts, just try NOT automating it… You will soon discover that hand crafting is not the point of the game (see point 1). Automate 1 or 2 factories for everything in the game. 3 or 4 for really common things like belts. Obviously science needs many more but you don't need 6 factories making drills or even blue insurers. If you can't bring yourself to do that then make a list of things you run out of in the middle of a project and automate those. Usually the list of things you don't need automated is shorter than the list of things you do need. This is why the lazy bastard achievement is one of the most recommended playthroughs for new players. Get out of the mindset of hand crafting. There are few things as wonderful as having 2 or 3 stacks of the exact thing you need waiting for you when you need them. And once you get red chests, you'll not even have to go around the best. It can come to you.

# Goal is to have fun

There are no wrong ways to play if you're having fun. These suggestions increase the chances of having fun.
