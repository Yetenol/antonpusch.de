# Blueprints

## Item provider station

### Goals

- Inputs **nine** lanes of items
- Outputs **three** 1+3 trains
- **Distribute** the output **evenly** by balancing trains
- Ensure **throughput** by loading from 3 stops in parallel
- Avoid **idling trains** and simplify conditions in train routines by enabling the stop only when all wagons of a train could be fully loaded from the buffer chests
- **Visualize bottlenecks** with colored lights that indicate fill levels of buffer chests
