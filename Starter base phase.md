# Starter base

Design structure and layout of the base

Use a 100x100 city block design
- scaling of raw materials and scaling of production are independent
- One task per block
- Structures main bus design
- easy expansion and high readability

Make big smelting columns
- start with stone furnaces, upgrade on the go
- connect raw ores to the smelters

Green circuit block
- leave plenty of space for expansion

Science block
- put labs in another block

First production blocks
- Green circuit, science, hub

Hub block
- replaces jump start base
- Builds and inserters
- assemblers and power poles
- pipes and trains (and later uranium power)

Train in more resources
- use blueprints for train intersections and co

- Mine starter ore patches
- Assemble basic building blocks with hand-feed input chests
    - Belts, Inserters, Power poles, Assembling machines, Lamps
- Smelt iron, copper with expandable setup
- Automate and stockpile basic building blocks
- Research red and green science

Main bus

- Expand starter base if possible
- Align and separate each component into its own city block
    - 1 block column for the resource bus
    - 1 block column for future trains
    - 1 block column for the fluid bus
    - blocks to the right or left of the main bus for components
    - 1 block for smelting above the main bus
- Create a resource bus for basic resources
    - 4 lanes of iron plates
    - 4 lanes of copper plates
    - 4 lanes of green circuit
    - 2 lanes of steel
    - 2 lanes of coal
    - 2 lanes of stone
    - 2 lanes of brick
    - 2 lanes of plastic
    - 2 lanes of batteries
    - 2 lanes of engines
    - 2 lanes of electric engines
    - 2 lanes of iron ore
    - 4 lanes of red circuits
    - 4 lanes of blue circuits

Roadmap

1. Mall
2. Main bus
3. Train network

4. Assemble belts, inserters and assembling machines with hand-feed input chests
5. Smelt iron
6. Automate power poles, etc.
7. Create a **Mall** of all items you want to build
8. Explore new resource patches
9. Produce a main bus of iron plates, copper plates, stone
10. Improve automation of inserters. belt, etc.
11. Automate science
12. Produce power
13. Research explosive tank shells
14. Add double lane train tracks to outposts
15. Provide smelting station
16. Provide green circuit station
17. Research robots
18. Create a base-wide logistics network for personal logistics and construction bots
