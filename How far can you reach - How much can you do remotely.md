The physicality of your factory is a meaningful part its design process. While in the early stages you can only see your unlit surroundings, with research comes lighting at night, further visibility with radar systems and remote control within the construction robot network or with spidertrons. On the one hand, construction robots make large construction jobs much less tedious, while on the other, pipes and tightly packed machines make you rethink your designs. It's not fun to play without radar systems, but creating infinitely expanding spaghetti monsters in the map editor isn't the goal either.
Superreach can reduce the sense of progress and having to walk to the middle of a city block frustrates. So I divide into different sizes here.

# Concepts

Should you be able to build everywhere in your field of view?

- [x] Moving the character closer several times while placing a row of inserters is annoying

Should you be able to build everywhere covered by radar?

- [x] In the map view, Blueprints can already create ghosts and paste somewhat awkwardly copied configurations

Should you be able to offensively place turrets right next to enemies at a safe distance?

- [ ] This breaks many combat mechanics
