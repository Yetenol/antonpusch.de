> [!quote]- My Feedback
> I want to thank the developers of this app for creating such an outstanding launcher. I'm extremely impressed. It makes playing and managing modded minecraft easier and more effortless by an order of magnitude. I used to use CurseForge, but they have fewer mod that Modrinth, their app is slow to launch, the UI is cluttered, and most annoyingly launching a game still opens the default Minecraft launcher where I constantly had to sign in again. The Modrinth app solves all there problems and is better that I could have imagined a launcher to be. It opens instantly, the UI is clean, straightforward, and a beauty to behold. Starting a profile launches Minecraft directly. I am very pleased. To be honest before Modrinth app existed, I was a bit confused, how I were supposed to use the Modrinth website. Downloading and updating files and their dependencies manually seems annoying and prone to do mistakes, and cloning each mod as a git repository seems even worse. Since the launch of the Modrinth app, I am more motivated to play Minecraft, and to try out new mods. Thank you from the bottom of my heart. I'm financially stressed a the moment, but when I can afford it, I will donate.
