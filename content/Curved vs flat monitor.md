# Curved monitor

![](./attachments/Pasted%20image%2020221230113623.png)

- more **immersive experience**
- **less distortion** particularly at the edges
- **more comfortable** for your eyes without strain
- larger perceived **field of view**

# Flat monitor

- easy and flush **wall mounting**
- **less glare** due to its simple geometry
