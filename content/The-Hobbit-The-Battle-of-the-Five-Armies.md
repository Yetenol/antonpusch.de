[The Hobbit: The Battle of the Five Armies](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt2310332) is a 2014 movie about Adventure, Fantasy. It is  from New Zealand, the United States directed by [Peter Jackson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Jackson) written by [Fran Walsh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fran_Walsh), [Philippa Boyens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philippa_Boyens), [Peter Jackson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Jackson) starring [Ian McKellen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ian_McKellen), [Martin Freeman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin_Freeman), [Richard Armitage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Armitage).
