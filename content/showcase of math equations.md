Let $f = x^2 + \frac{1}{11}$:
$$
\begin{align*} \qquad&\hspace{-2em}
\mathbb{P}(X+Y=k) 
= \sum_{\mathclap{x \in X(\Omega)}} \mathbb{P}(X = x) \cdot \mathbb{P}(Y=k-x) \\&
= \sum_{x = 0}^n \binom{n}{x}\, p^x\, (1-p)^{n-x} \cdot \binom{m}{k-x}\, q^{k-x}\, (1-q)^{m-(k-x)}
\end{align*}
$$
