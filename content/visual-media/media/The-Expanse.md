[The Expanse](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt3230854) is a television series releases from 2015–2022 about Drama, Mystery, Sci-Fi. It is  from the United States written by [Daniel Abraham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Abraham), [Mark Fergus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mark_Fergus), [Ty Franck](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ty_Franck) starring [Steven Strait](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steven_Strait), [Dominique Tipper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dominique_Tipper), [Wes Chatham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wes_Chatham).
