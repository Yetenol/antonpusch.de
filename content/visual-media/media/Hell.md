[Hell](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt1643222) is a 2011 movie about Horror, Sci-Fi, Thriller. It is  from Germany, Switzerland directed by [Tim Fehlbaum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tim_Fehlbaum) written by [Tim Fehlbaum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tim_Fehlbaum), [Oliver Kahl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oliver_Kahl), [Thomas Wöbke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thomas_Wöbke) starring [Lilo Baur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lilo_Baur), [Marco Calamandrei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marco_Calamandrei), [Lisa Vicari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lisa_Vicari).
