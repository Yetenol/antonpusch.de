[Pride & Prejudice](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0414387) is a 2005 movie about Drama, Romance. It is  from France, United Kingdom, the United States directed by [Joe Wright](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joe_Wright) written by [Deborah Moggach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deborah_Moggach), [Jane Austen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jane_Austen), [Emma Thompson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emma_Thompson) starring [Keira Knightley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keira_Knightley), [Matthew Macfadyen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matthew_Macfadyen), [Brenda Blethyn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brenda_Blethyn).

> Sparks fly when spirited Elizabeth Bennet meets single, rich, and proud Mr. Darcy. But Mr. Darcy reluctantly finds himself falling in love with a woman beneath his class. Can each overcome their own pride and prejudice? 

> The story is based on Jane Austen's novel about five sisters - Jane (Rosamund Pike), Elizabeth (Keira Knightley), Mary (Talulah Riley), Kitty (Carey Mulligan), and Lydia Bennet (Jena Malone) - in Georgian England. Their lives are turned upside down when wealthy young Mr. Bingley (Simon Woods) and his best friend, Mr. Darcy (Matthew Macfadyen), arrive in their neighborhood.
