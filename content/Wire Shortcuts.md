Wire Shortcuts is a [Factorio](./Factorio.md) extension about simplifier, circuit network. Removes red and green wires as craftable items and makes them available as shortcuts, similar to blueprints and planners. Let them clutter your inventory no more! Additionally, adds a shortcut to pick up a copper wire for the rare instances when it's necessary. Also includes a wire cutter selection tool to quickly remove all wires in an area.  
- Install extension via [Mod portal](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/WireShortcuts)
