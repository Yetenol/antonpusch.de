Auto Dark Mode is a [personalization](install%20personalization%20apps.md.md) app.  
- Open in [Microsoft Store](ms-windows-store://pdp/?ProductId=XP8JK4HZBVF435&mode=mini) or invoke:
  ```
  winget install -e XP8JK4HZBVF435 --accept-package-agreements
  ```
- Download the [latest release](https://github.com/AutoDarkMode/Windows-Auto-Night-Mode/releases/latest) from [Github](https://github.com/AutoDarkMode/Windows-Auto-Night-Mode)


- launch the application

> Open `Time`
> - `From sunset to sunrise (geographic coordinates)` =: Time
> - `52.52` =: Latitude _# Geographic coordinates_
> - `13.405` =: Longitude _# Geographic coordinates_

> Open `Settings`
> - [x] Hide tray icon (not recommended)
> - `English (English)` =: Language
