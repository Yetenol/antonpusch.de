Alternative names for the Internet
- *The Worldwide Web*
- *Mine of Informationen*
- *Informationen Mesh*

> So much of the internet has been branded, what’s interesting now is what different brands end up with as metaphors.
- Cohen

Remote computing industry
- Amazon called its infrastructure *Cloud* (2006)
- Reverse chronological feeds were called *Stream* (2009)
- Twitter was called *global town square* (2011)

Internet metaphors form the perception of concept but can also limit its use
- Gmail broke Email's mail metaphor with tags instead of physical folders


Metaphors about information are particularly powerful, as information is fairly formless
- Facebook redefined the terms
    - *Friendship* for any connection
    - *Newspaper* for its home feed
- Twitter is a global town square
