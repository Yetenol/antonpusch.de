Microsoft OneNote also called OneNote for Windows 10 is a **discarded** [Microsoft 365](./Microsoft%20365.md) extension. OneNote is your digital notebook for capturing and organizing everything across your devices. Jot down your ideas, keep track of classroom and meeting notes, clip from the web, or make a to-do list, as well as draw and sketch your ideas.    
- Install extension via [Microsoft Store](ms-windows-store://pdp/?ProductId=9WZDNCRFHVJL), [Webstore](https://microsoft.com/store/apps/9WZDNCRFHVJL)  
- Open in [Microsoft Store](ms-windows-store://pdp/?ProductId=9WZDNCRFHVJL&mode=mini), show in [webstore](https://microsoft.com/store/apps/9WZDNCRFHVJL) or invoke:  
  ```  
  winget install -e 9WZDNCRFHVJL --accept-package-agreements  
  ```  
  
  
- Click the three dots in the upper-right corner  
- Open `Settings > Options`  
  - [x] Sync down all files and images  
  - [x] Hide spelling errors  
  - `Merge Formatting` =: Paste Options
