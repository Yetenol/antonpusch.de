Almost Invisible Electric Wires is a [Factorio](./Factorio.md) extension about visual, circuit network. You can make the wires 80%, 50% or completely invisible. The Red and Green wire can be Blue and Yellow, or invisible too. Now you can customize it in settings.    
- Install extension via [Mod portal](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/AlmostInvisibleElectricWires)
