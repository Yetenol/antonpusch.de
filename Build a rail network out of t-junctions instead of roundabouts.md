> There's been lots of talk about the roundabout here, you might want to search the forum a bit to discover its disadvantages. The most significant disadvantage is that trains may reroute when passing a signal, and then they may reroute through themselves, causing the train to stop (no clear path) and get stuck forever in the roundabout (aka "deadlock"). You can solve this by not adding any signals within the loop, but that will obviously also greatly reduce throughput as only one train may use the roundabout at a time.
> 
> I designed a "squarabout" that aimed to solve the deadlock problem and improve throughput, but someone discovered that it's actually possible to deadlock (although it's extremely unlikely to happen).
> 
> In the end you're probably better off with this 4-way junction which I believe /u/RedditNamesAreShort designed. It does have a larger footprint than both the roundabout and the squarabout, but space tends to not be a significant issue. It allows better throughput than both a roundabout and the squarabout (by allowing two opposing trains to turn left at the same time) and it won't cause a deadlock. It doesn't offer U-turns, but this is easily solvable by adding a loop on one of the exits that's large enough to fit a train.
